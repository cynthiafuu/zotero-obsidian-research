# Purple annotation review (monthly)

Attach `70-Meta/exports/purple-<date>.md` and paste this.

---

These are all the doubts I flagged across N accounting/finance papers.
Purple = something I didn't buy or couldn't work out at the time.

1. Find recurring patterns — which kinds of doubt show up across
   multiple papers, and which papers each time.

2. Sort them into two buckets:
   (a) I misread it — tell me what to go read to fix that
   (b) the literature genuinely hasn't settled it

3. From bucket (b), pick at most three that could plausibly carry a paper.
   For each, state: what data it needs, what the identification strategy
   might be, and what the biggest obstacle is.

4. Point out which of my doubts already have answers in the literature,
   and name the paper I should read.

Do not reassure me. Do not treat every item as promising.
In my experience most of these land in bucket (a) — if that's what the
evidence shows, say so. If none of them clear the bar for a paper,
say that directly rather than promoting the least weak one.
