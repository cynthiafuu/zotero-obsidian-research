Below are the highlights I pulled from one accounting/finance paper,
grouped by color:

- **Yellow** = main finding
- **Red** = identification strategy
- **Green** = reusable method
- **Blue** = institutional background
- **Purple** = my own doubts

<paste highlights here>

Do three things. Do not summarize the paper back to me.

1. In two sentences, state exactly what variation the identification
   rests on, and what the single biggest threat to that variation is.

2. Sort my purple doubts into two buckets:
   (a) I misread something — tell me which section to reread
   (b) the literature genuinely hasn't settled it

3. If I wanted to extend this paper, what is the cheapest direction
   that could still make a real contribution, and what data would it need?

If the paper's contribution is actually thin, say so plainly.
Don't soften it and don't look for something nice to say.
