# Before writing a literature review

Attach every note in one stream, then paste this.

---

Attached are all my notes for one literature stream.

1. Lay them out as a line of development: who proposed what, who pushed
   back, who refined it, and where the debate currently sits.

2. Name the papers that are cited repeatedly across these notes but that
   I have no note for — those are probably classics I've skipped.

3. Draft the section structure for the literature review: headings and
   the claim each paragraph should make. Structure and claims only —
   do not write the prose for me.

4. Check whether any of my `finding` fields contradict each other.
   If they do, tell me which two papers to go re-read.

Refer to papers by citekey, never by full author names.
